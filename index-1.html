What can I create a QR code for?

Yes. QRCraft includes options for colors, size, logo, error correction, frame and QR style.

Does QRCraft work on mobile?

Yes. The interface is designed to adapt to phones, tablets and desktop screens.

About QRCraft

QRCraft is a simple, fast and easy-to-use QR code generator. Create beautiful, customizable QR codes for websites, text, Wi-Fi, email, contacts and more — all directly in your browser, with no account or login required.

Features

Everything you need to create great QR codes.

Fast & Simple

Generate a QR code in seconds. No registration, no setup, no waiting.

Mobile Friendly

Works beautifully on phones, tablets and desktops of any size.

Privacy Friendly

Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is ever uploaded to any server.

Free to Use

100% free with no hidden fees, no ads, and no limits on QR codes created.
